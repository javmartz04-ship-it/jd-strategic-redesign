The story

Second generation. Both sides of the deal.

“I’ve lived every side of it, the good deals and the bad ones.”

  1. The family

    Independent restaurants

    My family started as independent restaurant owners in hospitality. No system, no playbook, no brand behind us, and no way to build the systems to escape the daily operations.

  2. Franchisee

    The turn

    Skeptical of franchising for years, then a second conversation with a dry-cleaning brand changed it. My family became franchisees, and ten years later they are still multi-unit owners.

  3. High school

    The seed

    I managed one of the locations in high school and noticed the owner made money without being there. Ownership done right is not buying yourself a job.

  4. Franchisor

    The other side of the table

    We led the brand from ten to fifteen locations to more than 2,400 across the world. A strong franchisor has to be an expert in the industry and in franchising itself. That is the bar I hold every brand to.

  1. Finance

    The safe path

    I graduated early, went into finance and asset-management recruiting, and learned to evaluate businesses and people. I also saw people earning well over seven figures who still dreaded Monday. There is no safe path.

  2. The bet

    JD Strategic

    I went to the International Franchise Expo to buy a franchise and found franchise consulting instead. I took a six-figure loan, walked away from a remote six-figure salary, and started JD Strategic on nothing but belief in my own work ethic.

  3. Today

    Coach, and franchisee again

    A FranServe-certified franchise coach, working with about ten people a month as a free resource. And I am opening my own franchise in the Miami market, the first of a portfolio.

What I am building toward

Everything I do is built around three pillars.

Business ownership puts you in the driver’s seat of your life instead of the passenger seat.

Financial freedom
Equity in something you own, not a salary with an expiration date your boss never tells you about. Every job has one.
Lifestyle freedom
Being the CEO of the business, not the one stuck in it. My family runs four locations in Massachusetts from Florida. That is the model working.
A lasting legacy
Something to build on and something to pass down. Every family I help navigate this the right way now has both.
