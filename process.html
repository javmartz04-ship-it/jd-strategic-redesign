What we do with every client

A structured path from curious to confident owner.

Every step is a working session, not a sales call. Typically one to three months, at your pace, with one point of contact the whole way.

  1. 01

    Introductory call

    A short call to get the big picture.

    Where do you see yourself in three years, what is pulling you toward franchising, and what are you really after. By the end we know whether to move forward now, hold off, or that ownership is not the path. Any of those is a fine answer.

  2. 02

    Assessment and matching

    A brief assessment so I understand how you are wired: strengths, lifestyle, risk tolerance, market.

    I do the research, check territory availability, and present a tailored shortlist of vetted brands. Together we pick one to three to enter formal conversations with.

  3. 03

    Discovery calls with the franchisors

    You meet the brands directly, and I coach you through every call.

    We get into how the business really operates, what your day looks like as the owner, and the training, support, and marketing behind it. I send preparation materials before each one.

  4. 04

    Economics and franchisee validation

    The Franchise Disclosure Document, especially Item 19.

    The financial structure, the fees, your territory rights, the unit economics. Then direct calls with existing owners for the unfiltered version. Many candidates bring in a franchise attorney here, and I guide what to ask.

  5. 05

    Funding, Discovery Day, and your decision

    Some people move forward. Some do not. Either way, you leave with clarity.

    Funding partners are introduced in the background so you can compare SBA loans, ROBS, and lines of credit without being rushed. Then Discovery Day with the leadership and support teams.
